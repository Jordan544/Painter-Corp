Know Your Paint

Types of Paint in Uganda

Different paints for different surfaces. Here are the main types of paint used in Ugandan buildings and homes.

Interior Walls

Emulsion Paint

Water-based paint used for interior walls and ceilings. Easy to apply, quick-drying, and available in matte, silk, or satin finishes. The most common paint for homes in Uganda.

Wood & Metal

Gloss Paint

Oil-based paint with a shiny, hard-wearing finish. Used on doors, windows, frames, and metal surfaces. Easy to clean and resistant to moisture and stains.

Exterior Walls

Weatherguard / Exterior Paint

Tough, weather-resistant paint designed for outside walls. Protects against rain, sun, and dust. Essential for Ugandan homes exposed to harsh weather conditions.

Decorative

Texture / Decorative Paint

Thick paint used to create patterns, stucco effects, and decorative walls. Popular for feature walls, hotel lobbies, and modern homes in Kampala.

Preparation

Primer / Undercoat

The first coat applied before the main paint. It seals the surface, improves adhesion, and makes the topcoat last longer. Essential for new walls and repairs.

Specialty

Anti-Fungal / Anti-Mould Paint

Special paint that prevents mould, mildew, and fungi growth. Ideal for bathrooms, kitchens, and damp areas of the home. Very useful in Uganda's humid climate.

Roofing

Roof Paint

Heat-resistant paint for iron sheets and roof surfaces. Reflects sunlight, reduces indoor heat, and protects the roof from rust and corrosion.

Floors

Floor Paint

Hard-wearing paint for concrete, wooden, and cement floors. Used in garages, verandas, warehouses, and sometimes living spaces for a clean, finished look.
